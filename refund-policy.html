Refund & Cancellation Policy

This is the same cancellation, postponement, and deposit policy already published in our FAQ — collected here as one clear reference. It applies to all Social Events and Corporate bookings. Last updated: 31 August 2026.

1. Deposit & cleaning fee

All bookings require a refundable security deposit and a one-time, non-refundable cleaning fee, paid together with full rental payment to confirm your booking:

Social Events
$500 refundable deposit + $80 cleaning fee
Corporate
$200 refundable deposit + $50 cleaning fee

The cleaning fee is not refundable under any circumstances, as it covers post-event servicing of the venue regardless of outcome.

2. Cancellation

If you need to cancel your booking entirely, refunds of the rental fee are based on how far in advance you cancel, relative to your event date:

More than 6 weeks before the eventFull refund of rental fee
3 to 6 weeks before the event50% of rental fee forfeited
Less than 3 weeks before the eventNo refund of rental fee

The security deposit is handled separately — see Section 4.

3. Postponement

If you'd rather move your event to a new date than cancel outright:

More than 4 weeks before the eventOne complimentary date change (subject to availability)
4 weeks or less before the event$50 administrative fee applies

4. Deposit deductions & forfeiture

Your $500 (or $200 for Corporate) security deposit is separate from the cancellation terms above and is refunded in full within 5 working days after your event — provided there is no damage, excessive cleaning required, house rule breaches, or unreturned keys.

Deductions from the deposit may apply for:

  • Damage to equipment, furniture, or the venue
  • Excessive mess requiring additional cleaning beyond normal use
  • Unreturned keys or access devices
  • Fines and penalties for house rule breaches, as set out in our House Rules

If the police or any enforcement authority is activated or attends the venue due to noise complaints, illegal activity, disorderly conduct, or any breach of the House Rules caused by the Host, guests, or vendors, the full security deposit is forfeited — regardless of how long the authorities were present or whether the event was allowed to continue. If total fines or charges exceed the deposit amount, the Host is required to pay the balance.

5. How refunds are paid

Refunds are returned via the same payment method used for your original booking payment (e.g. bank transfer or PayNow), unless otherwise agreed with us in writing.

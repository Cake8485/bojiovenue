Cookie Policy

A short, honest explanation of cookie use on this website. Last updated: 31 August 2026.

1. What this website actually does

This website does not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising trackers, or any other analytics or marketing cookies. We don't track your browsing behaviour, build a profile of you, or share your activity on this site with advertisers.

Because of this, we don't show a cookie consent banner — there is nothing non-essential to ask your consent for. If that ever changes (for example, if we add analytics in future), we will update this policy and add a consent banner before any such cookie is set.

2. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file a website can ask your browser to store, typically used to remember your preferences, keep you logged in, or track your activity across visits. This site doesn't use logins or accounts, so most of the usual reasons for cookies don't apply here.

3. Third-party embeds

This site embeds one YouTube video (our venue tour) using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). In this mode, YouTube does not set cookies on your device unless and until you press play on the video. If you do play the video, YouTube's own cookie and privacy practices apply — see Google's Privacy Policy.

Our "Directions" page links out to Google Maps and our footer links to Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp — these are external sites you choose to visit, and their own cookie policies apply once you land on them. We don't control or receive data from those third-party sites.

4. Your browser controls

You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser's settings. Since this site sets no first-party tracking cookies, doing so won't affect how this website works for you.
